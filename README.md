**Salt Relay** is an offline 3D crime game set in Vesper Bay. Follow eight story jobs from Beacon Strand at sundown to the neon clubs of Mirage Row after dark. Slip past roadblocks and the patrol helicopter, or take on street races, boat slaloms, stunt jumps and courier runs. Earn respect to unlock jobs, weapons and the Tideglass S9. The pause atlas maps five districts and tracks career completion, standing and statistics. Save chapter checkpoints in three local slots and resume them from the title screen. Open `http://localhost:8080` in desktop Chrome. Move or drive with WASD or the arrows, sprint with Shift, brake with Space and interact or enter and exit vehicles with E. Hold the right mouse button to look or aim. Attack with the left mouse button or F, equip with 1–7 or Q and reload with R. Use V for radio, X to recover armor, Z for scanner blackout, B to slow a patrol car, M for the map and Esc to pause.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
